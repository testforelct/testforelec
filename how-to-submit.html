How to Submit

We are not accepting proposals yet. Please check back soon for the submission email address.

Here is a submission template. This template might not fit all proposals and can be adapted accordingly.

↓ Download the submission template (.docx)

Send your submission in Word format to: xxx@sciencespo.fr

Include the following in your email title: SUBELEC

Each proposal must designate a contact author. The submission must be sent from this person’s professional email. The contact author must be listed first on page 1 of the proposal. It is the person to whom all correspondence will be sent.

To preserve the anonymity of the review process, we ask that the main text of proposals (clearly marked in the template) be stripped of content that identifies the proposer(s). References to previous research that are stated in the third person are acceptable.

You can check the FAQ for more information.
