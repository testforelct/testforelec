Call for proposals

How to Submit

Here is a submission template. This template might not fit all proposals and can be adapted accordingly.

↓ Download the submission template (.docx)

Submit your proposal using the upload link below. We accept Word (.docx), OpenOffice (.odt), and Rich Text Format (.rtf).

Please name your file using the contact author’s name, as follows: lastname_firstname (for example, dupont_marie.docx).

When prompted, please enter your last name and first name again (in that order).

Upload your proposal →

If you cannot upload your proposal using the submission link, please email your proposal to: electrans-contact@univ-lille.fr. Include the following in your email title: [SUBM]

Each proposal must designate a contact author. The contact author must be listed first on page 1 of the proposal, with their professional email address. It is the person to whom all correspondence will be sent.

To preserve the anonymity of the review process, we ask that the main text of proposals (clearly marked in the template) be stripped of content that identifies the proposer(s). References to previous research that are stated in the third person are acceptable.

You can check the FAQ for more information.
