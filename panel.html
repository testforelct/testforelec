The Panel

ELECTRANS is a large longitudinal online panel that will follow approximately 10,000 respondents throughout the 2027 electoral cycle, beginning in January 2027. Ipsos, a leading French market research company, will be responsible for data collection.

We will share relevant documentation and technical information as it becomes available, including details on standard panel profile variables and sampling methodology.

Four survey waves are currently planned, beginning in January 2027. The exact fieldwork dates are still being determined.

Guidance for preparing your proposal: Please design your survey module as if no standard panel profile variables were available. If your proposal is selected, we will let you know which variables are already collected and therefore do not need to be included in your module.
