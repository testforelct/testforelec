ELECTRANS is a research project funded by the French National Research Agency (ANR) and devoted to the 2027 French elections.

ELECTRANS will conduct several major surveys surrounding the 2027 French elections. The goal is both to advance research on electoral behavior and political attitudes and to create a shared research infrastructure that can be used by the broader scientific community.

As part of this commitment, ELECTRANS has launched an open call for question proposals. Researchers are invited to propose survey questions, question batteries, modules, or experimental designs for possible inclusion in the 2027 ELECTRANS surveys.

Depending on their design and feasibility, selected proposals may be included in one or more waves of the ELECTRANS surveys. Selected questions will be fielded at no cost to the researchers submitting them. Data produced through ELECTRANS will subsequently be made available to the scientific community in accordance with the project’s open-science and data-protection policies.

We welcome proposals that address theoretically or substantively important research questions. Researchers may propose (this list is not exhaustive):

  • individual survey questions;
  • short batteries of related questions;
  • multi-item measures or scales;
  • repeated questions intended to track change over the course of the campaign;
  • survey experiments, including vignette-based designs;
  • questions designed to be combined with other measures collected by ELECTRANS.

For more information on the data collection, on how to submit, and for responses to the most commonly asked questions, please check the relevant tabs.
